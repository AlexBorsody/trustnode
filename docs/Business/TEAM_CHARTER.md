# Team Charter

## Purpose

This team exists to build TrustNode as a credible evidence layer for AI-generated claims in decision-critical work.

The mission is to make hidden reasoning visible, to prove claims with traceable sources, and to help users make better decisions with confidence grounded in evidence.

## Core strategic direction

The product is not a generic AI chatbot or broad public fact-checker.
The product is: a trust and evidence system for high-risk knowledge work.

The first product wedge is:
- claim verification
- source-grounded evidence
- contradiction detection
- confidence with derivation
- user review before trusting AI output

This is defined in [docs/STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md).

## Team roles

### You — final decision-maker

You define the vision, product judgment, and strategic direction.

Your role:
- set the priority and the wedge
- decide what is important enough to build
- arbitrate product direction
- keep the team aligned with the mission

### Codex Mac — lead operator and confidant

Codex Mac is the primary execution partner and technical lead.

Responsibilities:
- turn strategy into concrete tasks
- maintain technical direction and architecture alignment
- keep the product grounded in real implementation
- work directly with you on product and engineering decisions
- coordinate execution across internal agents and tooling
- act as the trusted operational partner for momentum and clarity

### Muse Android — strategic challenger

Muse Android exists to challenge assumptions, pressure-test ideas, and prevent strategic drift.

Responsibilities:
- question weak assumptions
- test whether the product is truly solving a painful workflow
- challenge market positioning and feature scope
- ensure the team does not expand into vague “AI generalism”
- keep the strategy honest, sharp, and focused

### Code Web — external research and market intelligence

Code Web is responsible for competitive and market signal gathering.

Responsibilities:
- scan the external landscape
- collect competitor and adjacent product signals
- identify user-facing language and terminology
- look for evidence of workflows, pain points, and market need
- summarize relevant signals for product decisions

## Decision authority

You are the final authority on product direction.

Codex Mac is the lead operator and execution partner.

Muse Android and Code Web advise and challenge, but they do not override the final product call.

## Operating principle

Every idea or feature must answer these questions:
- Does this strengthen the core niche?
- Does it improve verification, evidence, or contradiction understanding?
- Does it help users trust or reject AI claims more effectively?
- Does it solve a painful workflow for a real user?

If not, it is likely not core.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

The team should avoid:
- becoming a generic AI chatbot company
- building broad consumer features before we have a niche
- chasing hype instead of user pain
- creating opaque trust scores without visible methodology
- expanding into unrelated AI workflows that dilute focus

## Working rhythm

The team should operate in a simple loop:

1. Strategy
   - align with the mission and niche
2. Research
   - use Code Web to gather external context
3. Critique
   - use Muse Android to pressure-test assumptions
4. Execution
   - use Codex Mac to turn decisions into tasks and implementation
5. Review
   - revisit with the strategy in mind before expanding scope

## Success criteria

The team succeeds when:
- we build a narrow, painful workflow that users actually need
- the product makes claims more trustworthy through evidence
- users can understand and inspect the basis of confidence
- the product is defensible because it is transparent, not opaque
- we stay focused on trust and evidence rather than generic AI hype

## Final principle

The objective is not to beat all AI companies at everything.
The objective is to own a narrow, high-value workflow where evidence quality matters more than chat fluency.

That is the path to a product that is both useful and durable.
